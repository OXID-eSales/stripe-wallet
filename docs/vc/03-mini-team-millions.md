## 💡 Mini-Team Success Principles

<div class="grid-2">

<div>

### DO's ✅
- **Equal splits** (avoid future fights)
- **Weekly syncs** (stay aligned)
- **Written agreements** (document everything)
- **Clear domains** (own your area)
- **Celebrate wins** (builds morale)
- **Hire support early** (don't do customer service yourselves)
- **Reinvest 20%** (into marketing/growth)

</div>

<div>

### DON'Ts ❌
- **Don't skip legal** (founder agreements!)
- **Don't micromanage** (trust your teammates)
- **Don't overbuild** (ship MVP fast)
- **Don't ignore customers** (feedback = gold)
- **Don't burn out** (sustainable pace)
- **Don't fight equity** (fair is fair)
- **Don't delay decisions** (velocity matters)

</div>

</div>

<div class="success">
<strong>Mini-team golden rule:</strong> Treat teammates as co-owners (they are!), not employees. Mutual respect = mutual success.
