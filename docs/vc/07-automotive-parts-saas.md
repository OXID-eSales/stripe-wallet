## Problem: Automotive Aftermarket Is Stuck in the 1990s

<div class="feature-box">
<h3>The Current Reality</h3>
<p>Over <strong>70% of independent garages</strong> source parts through <strong>wholesalers</strong> (CLEPA data). Yet the ordering process is painfully analog.</p>
</div>

<div class="grid-2">

<div>

### For Repair Shops (Buyers)

**Pain Points:**
- ❌ Phone calls to 5-10 distributors for price checks
- ❌ No real-time inventory visibility
- ❌ Wrong parts ordered (fitment errors)
- ❌ 20-30% of time wasted on parts procurement
- ❌ No price comparison = overpaying

**Impact:**
- Lost productivity: 5-8 hours/week per mechanic
- Wrong parts: 15% of orders need returns
- Margin erosion: Overpaying 10-25% vs market

</div>

<div>

### For Distributors (Sellers)

**Pain Points:**
- ❌ Phone/fax orders = manual data entry
- ❌ No digital catalog (just PDF price lists)
- ❌ Can't reach new customers online
- ❌ Price updates = call every customer
- ❌ Losing market share to Amazon Business

**Impact:**
- High operational costs: €150K-300K/year for order processing
- Customer churn: 15-20%/year to digital-first competitors
- Limited growth: Can't scale beyond local region

</div>

</div>

<div class="highlight">
<strong>The market is ripe for disruption:</strong> Germany's e-commerce automotive aftermarket captured 22.6% share in 2024 and growing at 9.1% CAGR (fastest in B2B!)
</div>
