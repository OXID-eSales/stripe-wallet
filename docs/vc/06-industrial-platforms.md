## Product #4: Invoice & Credit Management Module

**The Problem:**
- B2B customers expect "net 30/60/90" payment terms (not pay-upfront like B2C)
- OXID has basic invoice functionality, but no credit management
- Shop owners manually track credit limits in Excel
- No automated credit hold when limit exceeded
- No dunning process (overdue payment reminders)
- Accounting chaos = late payments

**Your Solution: B2B Credit Management**
- Credit limit per customer (admin-configurable)
- Real-time credit utilization tracking
- Automatic order hold when limit exceeded
- Payment terms (net 7/14/30/60/90 days)
- Dunning process (automated overdue reminders)
- Invoice consolidation (weekly/monthly)
- Accounting export (DATEV, SAP, etc.)
