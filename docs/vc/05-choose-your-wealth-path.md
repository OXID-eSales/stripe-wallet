## 🎯 Decision Framework: Which Path Is Right for You?

<div class="grid-2">

<div>

### Choose TRADITIONAL if:
✅ You value stability above all
✅ Risk terrifies you
✅ You have family obligations
✅ You don't want to lead
✅ You prefer being an IC
✅ You're close to retirement

**Reality:** €90K/year for life. Comfortable, but not wealthy. No regrets if you're happy!

### Choose SOLO if:
✅ You crave independence
✅ You hate meetings & politics
✅ You're a generalist (full-stack+)
✅ You can work alone for months
✅ You want 100% ownership
✅ You're disciplined & self-motivated

**Reality:** €500K-1M/year Year 2-3. Financial freedom, but lonely grind.

</div>

<div>

### Choose MINI-TEAM if:
✅ You have 1-2 trusted developer friends
✅ You want to share the journey
✅ You value collaboration
✅ You can split ownership fairly
✅ You want medium risk/reward
✅ You're a T-shaped specialist

**Reality:** €1-3M/year Year 3. Best risk/reward ratio. Strong friendships = success.

### Choose PLATFORM if:
✅ You're a senior/lead developer
✅ You have 8+ years experience
✅ You want massive impact
✅ You can coordinate teams
✅ You're comfortable with complexity
✅ You want generational wealth

**Reality:** €5-25M/year Year 3. Life-changing, but high stakes & coordination.

</div>

## 🎭 Lifestyle Comparison: How You'll Actually Live

<div class="grid-2">

<div>

### Traditional Life
**Work:** 9-6, Mon-Fri, meetings
**Stress:** Low-medium (deadlines)
**Freedom:** Limited (vacation, PTO)
**Wealth:** Comfortable (not rich)
**Legacy:** Code that gets rewritten

**Day-to-day:**
- Morning commute or Zoom standup
- Write code per specs
- Attend meetings (2-4 hours/day)
- Work/life balance: Good
- Evenings/weekends: Free!

**Verdict:** Stable, predictable, safe. You'll never be rich, but you'll never starve.

</div>

<div>

### Solo Life
**Work:** Your schedule (often 60-80h weeks Year 1)
**Stress:** High early, low later
**Freedom:** Ultimate (work anywhere)
**Wealth:** Rich (€1-2M/year by Year 3)
**Legacy:** Product with your name on it

**Day-to-day:**
- Wake up when you want
- Code 6-10 hours/day (no meetings!)
- Customer support (1-2 hours)
- Marketing (1-2 hours)
- Work/life balance: You control it
- Evenings/weekends: Often working Year 1-2, then free

**Verdict:** Hard early grind, amazing later. Lonely but lucrative. Digital nomad dream.

</div>

</div>

## ⚡ Decision Time: Your Personal Quiz

**Answer these 7 questions honestly:**

1. **Risk tolerance:** Can you handle 6-12 months with zero income?
   - No → Traditional · Yes → Continue

2. **Savings:** Do you have €50-100K saved or access to it?
   - No → Traditional or find investors · Yes → Continue

3. **Social needs:** Can you work alone for months without going crazy?
   - No → Mini-Team or Platform · Yes → Solo or Continue

4. **Experience:** 8+ years as developer, shipped production systems?
   - No → Solo or Mini-Team · Yes → Platform is an option

5. **Network:** Do you have 2-5 trusted developer friends you'd partner with?
   - No → Solo · Yes → Mini-Team or Platform

6. **Ambition:** Do you want €5M+/year or is €1M enough?
   - €1M is enough → Solo or Mini-Team · €5M+ → Platform

7. **Leadership:** Comfortable coordinating 5-6 people, making hard calls?
   - No → Solo or Mini-Team · Yes → Platform
