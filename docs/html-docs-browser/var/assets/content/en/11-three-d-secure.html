Features · 6 of 7 · 3D Secure / SCA
PSD2 compliance — without code on our side

3D Secure is Stripe’s problem.
We just keep the contract in sync.

PSD2 mandates Strong Customer Authentication for most EU payments. Stripe Checkout handles the SCA challenge inline — the customer is already redirected, and the bank’s 3DS page renders inside Stripe’s domain. Our job is to react to the few outcomes that matter for the contract.

The four outcomes we model
Stripe outcomeWhat we do
SCA passed · succeededcontract → READY_TO_COMMIT
SCA required at return · requires_actionredirect to next_action.redirect_to_url
SCA failed · payment_failedcontract → FAILED; order stays NOT_FINISHED
SCA timed out · requires_payment_methodreturn customer to cl=order with toast

What stays in the contract
  • The contract stays in PENDING while SCA is in progress. Not AUTHORIZED — the bank hasn’t agreed yet.
  • The BasketSnapshot guarantees the price the customer saw is the price they’re asked to authenticate — no in-flight changes.
  • If SCA fails permanently, the order remains NOT_FINISHED and is reaped by the housekeeping job after 24h.
  • Recovery email links the customer back to the same contract — no duplicate orders.
