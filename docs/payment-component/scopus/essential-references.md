## Notes for Article Writing

### Literature Review Structure

**Standard Pattern:**
1. **Historical Foundation** (older citations)
   - McCabe [1] introduced cyclomatic complexity in 1976...

2. **Empirical Validation** (2000-2015)
   - Subsequent studies validated the complexity-defect relationship [2, 3, 8, 9]...

3. **Recent Advances** (2015-present)
   - Recent work extends these findings to security vulnerabilities [4, 12, 14, 16]...

4. **Gaps in Literature** (what we add)
   - However, no study has examined complexity in payment systems...
   - Our work addresses this gap by...

### Citation Density
- **Introduction:** 5-8 citations/page
- **Literature Review:** 15-25 citations/page
- **Methodology:** 2-5 citations/page (methods papers)
- **Discussion:** 8-12 citations/page (compare to prior work)

### Common Phrases
- "Building on the work of McCabe [1], we..."
- "Consistent with prior research [2, 4, 7], our findings show..."
- "In contrast to Smith et al. [10], we found that..."
- "This extends the 'Accelerate' research [25] by..."
