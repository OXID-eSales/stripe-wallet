## Presentation Tips

### Timing (Total: 20-25 minutes)
- Slides 1-4: 5 minutes (setup)
- Slides 5-7: 4 minutes (numbers)
- Slides 8-9: 3 minutes (**KEY SLIDES**)
- Slides 10-11: 2.5 minutes (practical)
- Slides 12-14: 3 minutes (strategy)
- Slides 15-16: 2.5 minutes (execution)
- Slides 17-18: 2.5 minutes (close)
- Q&A: 5-10 minutes

### Body Language
- **Stand up.** Never sit during this presentation.
- **Move.** Walk to the screen for key points.
- **Pause.** After big numbers, pause for 2-3 seconds.
- **Eye contact.** Especially on Slides 9, 17, 18.
- **Hand gestures.** Use them for emphasis, not distraction.

### Voice
- **Vary your pace.** Slow for big numbers. Fast for exciting parts.
- **Vary your volume.** Whisper for "€8.6 million." Shout for "Let's build the future."
- **Pause strategically.** Silence is powerful.

### Energy Arc
- Start: 8/10 energy (confident, not manic)
- Middle: 6/10 (informative, steady)
- Slide 9: 10/10 (peak emotion)
- Slide 16: 5/10 (calm, rational)
- Closing: 9/10 (triumphant)

### Handling Questions
- **"This seems too good to be true."** → Show the data. Reference successful platforms (Shopify, Atlassian).
- **"What if it fails?"** → Point to Slide 16. GO/NO-GO at Month 6. Only €600K at risk.
- **"Why hasn't anyone done this before?"** → They have (in the US). Europe is 5 years behind. We're catching up.
- **"What do you need from us?"** → Commitment. 6 months. All-in. Then you're wealthy.
