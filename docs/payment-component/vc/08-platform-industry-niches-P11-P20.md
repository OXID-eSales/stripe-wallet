## REVENUE SHARING MODEL

### The OSC Ecosystem Chain

```
Merchant/Client → Agency → Mini-Team → OSC → OXID AG
```

### Standard Revenue Distribution

For each platform product, we use the following **revenue sharing model**:

```
Gross Revenue:                    100%
├─ Agency Commission:              15-20% (if agency partner involved)
├─ Net Platform Revenue:           80-85%
    ├─ OXID AG Partnership:         5% (ecosystem support)
    ├─ OSC Infrastructure:         10% (hosting, legal, admin)
    └─ Mini-Team (Developers):     65-70%
        ├─ Founding Developers:     60% (royalty, lifetime)
        ├─ Operations/Support:      25% (ongoing costs)
        └─ Growth Fund:             15% (R&D, expansion)
```

### Developer Royalty Structure

**Key Innovation:** Developers who build the product receive **lifetime royalties** on every transaction.

- **Founding Team:** 60% of mini-team profits (distributed proportionally)
- **Vesting:** No vesting - royalties start immediately
- **Duration:** Lifetime of product (even if developer leaves)
- **Transferable:** Can be sold/transferred (liquidity event)

**Example:** Developer builds product generating €1M net revenue
- Mini-team share: €700K (70% of €1M)
- Founding developers: €420K (60% of €700K)
- If 3 developers: €140K per developer per year

This creates **aligned incentives** - developers benefit from long-term product success.

## KEY INSIGHTS: REVENUE SHARING MODEL

### Developer Incentive Alignment

**The OSC model creates powerful incentives:**

1. **Lifetime Royalties:** Developers earn continuously from products they build
2. **No Dilution:** Unlike equity, royalties don't dilute over time
3. **Passive Income:** Royalties continue even if developer leaves
4. **Scalable:** Income grows with product success
5. **Transferable:** Royalties can be sold (liquidity event)
