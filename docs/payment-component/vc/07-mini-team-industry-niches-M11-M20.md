## M11: USED MEDICAL EQUIPMENT MARKETPLACE

### Overview
B2B marketplace for buying/selling certified used medical equipment between hospitals, clinics, and dealers across East Europe, DACH, and Western Mediterranean.

### Regional Focus
- **Primary:** East Europe (Poland, Czech Republic, Romania, Bulgaria, Hungary)
- **Secondary:** DACH (Germany, Austria, Switzerland)
- **Tertiary:** Western Mediterranean (Italy, Spain, Portugal)

### Market Opportunity
- **Market Size:** €2.8B used medical equipment market (EU)
- **East Europe Growth:** 12% CAGR (hospitals upgrading)
- **Problem:** No trusted B2B platform, all sales via brokers (20-30% commission)
- **Target Customers:** 15,000+ hospitals/clinics in target regions

### Investment Analysis
```
Development Hours:
  Marketplace platform:      400 hours × €65 = €26,000
  Certification system:      150 hours × €65 = €9,750
  Regional integrations:     100 hours × €65 = €6,500
  Compliance (MDR/FDA):      100 hours × €65 = €6,500
  Multi-language (5 langs):   80 hours × €65 = €5,200
  Payment routing:            70 hours × €65 = €4,550

Total Development:           900 hours = €58,500
Marketing (regional):                   = €15,000
Operations setup:                       = €8,500
Total Investment:                       = €82,000
```

### Team Structure
- **Team Size:** 2-3 developers
- **Timeline:** 5-6 months
- **Required Skills:** E-commerce, certification systems, multi-language, EU medical device regulations

### Revenue Model
- **Transaction Fee:** 8% on GMV (lower than brokers' 20-30%)
- **Premium Listings:** €499-€2,499/month (featured equipment)
- **Certification Service:** €299-€999 per equipment (inspection/certification)
- **Financing Integration:** 2% on financed transactions

### Revenue Projections
