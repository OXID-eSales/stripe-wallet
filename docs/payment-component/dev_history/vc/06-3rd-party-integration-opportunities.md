**Problem:** Every OXID shop needs to connect to 5-15 external systems (ERP, CRM, shipping, accounting, marketing). Each integration costs €5K-€50K and takes 2-12 months.

**Market Size:**

**Revenue Models:**

## STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS

### Priority 1: ERP Integrations (Start Here!)
**Why:**
- ✅ Highest value (€23.3M Y3)
- ✅ Mandatory for B2B shops (must-have)
- ✅ High pricing (€5K-€50K projects)
- ✅ Long-term relationships (5+ years)
- ✅ Sticky (switching cost very high)

**Start With:**
1. SAP Business One (largest market share)
2. Microsoft Dynamics 365 (easier integration)
3. DATEV (German market GOLD MINE - mandatory!)

---

### Priority 2: Accounting Systems (Quick Win!)
**Why:**
- ✅ Mandatory (shops MUST have accounting)
- ✅ DATEV = German law requirement (captive market!)
- ✅ Recurring revenue (tax updates, compliance)
- ✅ Low competition (few OXID-DATEV connectors exist)

**Focus:** DATEV first (40% of German market, mandatory)

---

### Priority 3: CRM + Marketing (Volume Play)
**Why:**
- ✅ High adoption (every shop needs CRM/marketing)
- ✅ Lower complexity (easier APIs)
- ✅ Fast implementation (2-4 weeks vs 3-6 months for ERP)
- ✅ Multiple platforms (diversification)

**Start With:** Salesforce, HubSpot, Google Ads

---

### Priority 4: Logistics (Last)
**Why:**
- ⚠️ Commoditized (many shipping modules exist)
- ⚠️ Low margins (€49-€149/mo)
- ⚠️ High maintenance (carrier API changes)

**Strategy:** Build basic connectors, focus on premium features (rate shopping, returns)
